Post-Construction

We transform construction sites and chaotic renovations into sparkling, move-in-ready environments. Meticulous extraction of fine plaster dust, paint residues, and chemical splatters on newly built residential and commercial spaces.

What's Included:

  • ✨ Plaster & Drywall Dust Removal: Meticulous vacuuming and wiping of walls, ceilings, and fixtures.
  • ✨ Paint & Adhesive Extraction: Careful removal of paint drips, tape residues, and window labels.
  • ✨ Window & Frame Detailing: Cleaning interior glass, tracks, and frames of construction residue.
  • ✨ Hard Surface Buffing: Vacuuming, scrubbing, and buffing tile, wood, and concrete floors.
  • ✨ Safety Sanitization: Deep cleaning vents, registers, light switches, and built-in cabinetry.

What's Included:

  • ✨ Deep Appliance Cleaning: Full interior and exterior cleaning of ovens, refrigerators, and dishwashers.
  • ✨ Cabinet & Drawer Detailing: Inside and out washing and sanitizing of all cabinets and cupboards.
  • ✨ Sanitized Bathrooms: Heavy-duty soap scum removal, tile grout scrubbing, and mirror polishing.
  • ✨ Baseboards & Trim: Thorough hand-wiping of all doors, frames, switchplates, and baseboards.
  • ✨ Complete Floor Care: Wall-to-wall vacuuming, edging, and sanitizing of all hard floor surfaces.

Window Specialties

Restore natural light and impeccable curb appeal. We eliminate mineral dust, water spot etching, and weather residues from interior and exterior glass, rigid structures, and frames for residential properties and commercial offices.

What's Included:

  • ✨ Interior & Exterior Glass: Streak-free finish using safe, premium glass cleaners.
  • ✨ Screen & Frame Detailing: Brushing screen mesh, washing sills, tracks, and frame borders.
  • ✨ Hard Water Stain Removal: Special chemical treatment to lift stubborn mineral deposits.
  • ✨ High-Reach Cleaning: Using professional extensions and tools to safely clean upper-story windows.
  • ✨ Glass Protection Coating: Applying dust and rain repelling shield to keep windows clean longer.
